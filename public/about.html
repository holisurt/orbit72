A strategic technology

Satellite technology is on Vietnam's list of strategic technologies (Decision 1131/QĐ-TTg, 2025; 21/2026/QĐ-TTg). ORBIT 72 lets students feel the real trade-offs behind a rescue constellation.

Store-and-forward rescue

Each CubeSat picks up SOS messages from cut-off villages, stores them, and drops them at the Hòa Lạc ground station. The score is how often a message gets through in under an hour.

How to play

From idea to orbit in six phases

  1. Mission

    Accept the “72 Giờ Vàng” contract: eight requirements, a budget and an 18-month deadline.

  2. Build

    Fit computer, power, attitude control, radio and SOS payload into a 3U/6U/12U CubeSat. A live HUD shows mass, power, battery, data, cost and time.

  3. Orbit

    Pick inclination, altitude, planes and satellites per plane, and a rocket. See ground tracks, coverage gaps and link margins update.

  4. Go/No-Go

    Every role answers GO or NO-GO against requirements R1–R8. A NO-GO needs a reason.

  5. Operations

    Run the 72 hours after landfall: real rain over the map, a geomagnetic storm, a ground-station power cut, clouds — each with options you compare by numbers.

  6. Debrief

    Five stars, a why-chain (cause → effect → what to try), a spec sheet, a JPL-style Concept Maturity badge, and a shareable mission patch.

FAQ

Questions

Do I need to install anything?

No. It runs in any modern browser on a phone, tablet or PC. Landscape works best on phones.

Is it in English?

The game text is Vietnamese. Settings (gear icon) switches menus and buttons to English.

Can several people play together?

Yes — co-op for 2–4 players on one device, with roles (Flight Director, Satellite, Science, Orbit & Comms) and a pass-the-device screen.

Does it collect data?

No accounts and no tracking. Progress and quiz answers are saved only in your browser (localStorage), without names.

Are the numbers real?

Physics uses standard formulas; rain and space-weather data come from NASA; prices and some parameters are game assumptions, all listed in the Guide.
